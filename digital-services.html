Bulk SMS & Digital Services for Ghanaian Businesses

What the Platform Does

Bulk messaging, airtime and data, TV subscriptions and utility payments, managed from one account and one wallet.

Bulk SMS

Send single or bulk SMS, schedule campaigns, manage contacts and groups, use templates, and track delivery per recipient.

Airtime & Data

Buy airtime and data bundles for MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo and Vodafone directly from your wallet.

TV Subscriptions

Pay for DStv, GOtv and Startimes using a smart card number.

Utility Bills

Pay ECG (prepaid and postpaid) and Ghana Water bills from your wallet.

Bulk SMS in Detail

Everything you need to run a proper SMS campaign, from sender ID to delivery report.

Sender IDs

Register a custom Sender ID with supporting documents. It is reviewed by the Nedhub team and must be approved before you can send. You can resend a request if it is rejected.

Single & Bulk Send

Send to one recipient or to a list. Recipients are deduplicated, invalid numbers are filtered, and blacklisted numbers are skipped automatically.

Scheduling

Schedule a campaign for a future date and time. Funds are reserved at scheduling time and deducted when the campaign runs.

Resend Failed Messages

Any message that fails can be resent individually from your history, subject to available wallet balance.

Contact Groups & Import

Organise contacts into groups. Import contacts from CSV, Excel (XLS/XLSX), VCF or TXT files with automatic column detection, with a preview before import.

Templates & Personalisation

Save message templates and reuse them. Messages support personalisation (merge fields) so each recipient can receive a tailored message.

Opt-Out / Blacklist

Numbers can be added to a blacklist with a reason. Blacklisted numbers are excluded from sends automatically. You can remove a number from the blacklist at any time.

Delivery Reports & Analytics

Per-recipient status (sent, delivered, failed), campaign summaries, analytics charts and exportable reports (CSV/Excel) for your records.

Cost Calculator

Estimate the cost of a message before sending, based on recipient count and message length (segments). A maximum of 10 segments per message applies.

Message History

Searchable history of every message you have sent, with status, recipient, sender ID and timestamp.

Developer API

Send SMS programmatically from your own application.

  • Authentication: create API keys from your account dashboard. Keys use the prefix ndh_live_.
  • Usage: send SMS via the same platform pipeline, including sender ID validation, balance checks and delivery tracking.
  • Rate limit: default 60 requests per minute per key (configurable by the platform).
  • Management: list and revoke your API keys at any time.

API documentation is provided on the platform after you sign in. If you need integration help, contact us on WhatsApp.

Wallet & Payments

One wallet funds every service on the platform.

Top Up

Fund your wallet through the platform's secure checkout flow. You are redirected to the payment page and returned to the platform once payment is confirmed.

Balance & History

View your current balance, available balance, reserved amounts (for scheduled campaigns) and a full transaction history with reference numbers.

How Deductions Work

Each send or payment is checked against your available balance before it runs. If a provider call fails, the amount is restored to your wallet and the transaction is marked failed.

Confirmation

Airtime, data, TV and utility payments are confirmed by the payment provider. Status can be checked from your transaction history; pending transactions are reconciled automatically.

Responsible Use

SMS should only be sent to people who expect to hear from you.

  • Send only to recipients who have consented to receive your messages.
  • Honour opt-outs: numbers on your blacklist are excluded from every send.
  • Do not send spam, phishing, fraudulent or unlawful content, or content that breaches Ghanaian law or telecom rules.
  • Keep your Sender ID and account credentials secure and do not share them.
  • The platform deduplicates recipients and blocks blacklisted numbers automatically to reduce waste and complaints.

Security & Privacy

Factual, and only what the platform actually does.

Account Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use signed tokens, and API keys are stored hashed and can be revoked. Login and sensitive actions are protected.

Email Verification

New accounts verify their email address with a one-time code before activation. Password reset is also done by one-time code sent to your email.

Data Handling

Your contacts, messages and transaction records are stored against your account. We do not sell your data. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Audit Trail

Administrative actions such as wallet adjustments and Sender ID approvals are logged for accountability.
