Getting started

Most people are ready to send within one session.

  1. Create an account

    Register on the platform and confirm your email address.

  2. Request a sender ID

    Submit the name you want recipients to see. You can start planning your campaign while it is reviewed.

  3. Top up your wallet

    Add funds from your dashboard. The same balance pays for SMS, airtime, data, TV and bills.

  4. Send or pay

    Compose a campaign, buy a bundle or pay a bill, then check the status in your history.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start sending bulk SMS?

Create an account, confirm your email, top up your wallet and compose your first campaign. If you want your own sender name, request a sender ID first.

Can I import my contact list?

Yes. You can upload CSV or Excel files and organise your contacts into groups.

Can I personalise messages and save templates?

Yes. Messages can include details such as the recipient's name, and templates let you reuse wording you send often.

Can I schedule a campaign for later?

Yes. Choose a date and time and the campaign is sent automatically.

Do I need a sender ID?

A sender ID lets recipients see your name instead of a number. Each request is reviewed and approved before it can be used.

Which networks can I buy airtime and data for?

MTN, Telecel and AirtelTigo.

Which TV and utility payments are supported?

DStv, GOtv and Startimes subscriptions, plus ECG and Ghana Water bills.

What happens if an airtime, data or bill payment fails?

The amount is returned to your wallet, and the transaction shows as failed in your history. If something looks wrong, message us on WhatsApp with the transaction details.

Can I connect my own system?

Yes. You can create API keys in your account settings. Message us for the documentation.
